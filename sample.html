Why this is different

Your biodata should not keep circulating forever.

Traditional files are easy to forward, difficult to update, and impossible to revoke after downloading. A controlled portfolio gives families a clearer introduction without exposing everything at once.

Traditional biodata

Separate files and repeated follow-ups

  • PDFs, photos, and horoscope files are sent separately.
  • Old versions continue circulating after details change.
  • Personal contact details may be visible immediately.
  • Families have no clear way to manage genuine interest.
Nakshatra portfolio

One current profile with controlled access

Privacy by design

You decide what is shared and when.

Privacy is not a decorative feature added after launch. It is part of the profile, link, and introduction workflow from the beginning.

Public information Choose the basic details visible to anyone with the active link.
Restricted information Keep contact, full horoscope, and sensitive family details behind approval.
Private information Store account or internal information without displaying it to viewers.

Separate share links

Create different links for a WhatsApp group, relatives, or a direct introduction.

Expiry and revocation

Set an expiry date, pause a profile, or revoke a specific link when it is no longer needed.

Verified inquiries

Require phone verification before someone requests an introduction or restricted details.

Candidate-controlled approval

The candidate or authorized family member decides whether to approve, decline, or block a request.

Revoking a link prevents future access to the original portfolio. No website can completely prevent someone from copying or capturing information they were already allowed to view.
A clear next step

Move from profile sharing to a verified introduction.

The public viewer does not need an account. Verification begins only when someone requests an introduction or more information.

1. Open the link

The viewer sees the basic profile without creating an account.

2. Request introduction

The viewer provides their name, relationship, and prospect information.

3. Verify phone

A one-time code confirms the requester's phone number.

4. Candidate reviews

The candidate sees the verified request and any shared biodata or message.

5. Approve or decline

Restricted details are shared only when the candidate approves the introduction.
